EU Recovery: Largest Expansion Market Bouncing Back

EU DSR: $11.1M (2023) → $4.8M (2024, -56%) → $8.8M (2025, +82%)

EU remains the backbone of Expansion at 66% share. The 2024 decline was severe but 2025 shows strong recovery with launches back up (257→334). Still $2.3M below the 2023 peak — full recovery is the #1 priority for Expansion GMS growth.

JP DSR Paradox: More Launches, Less GMS

2025: 121 launches (+33%) but GMS $2.5M (-47% vs 2024's $4.7M)

JP DSR in 2024 was an anomaly — GMS spiked to $4.7M possibly due to a few high-performing sellers. 2025 reverted toward 2023 levels ($1.7M→$2.5M, +47% vs 2023) but with 26% more launches. The real question: was 2024 the outlier, or is 2025 underperforming?

AE: The Expansion Success Story of 2025

From near-zero to $964K with clear month-over-month ramp ($28K Jan → $142K Dec)

AE is the only Expansion market showing consistent acceleration. 96 DSR launches with GMS growing every quarter. If this trajectory holds, AE could surpass AU ($1.08M) in early 2026 and become the 3rd largest Expansion market.

Expansion Team Recommendations

1

EU: Close the Gap to 2023 Peak

EU is $2.3M below its 2023 peak ($11.1M vs $8.8M). Launches are recovering — focus on reactivating dormant 2023-cohort sellers and improving retention of 2024-2025 launches. Full recovery would lift Expansion share by ~4pp.

2

JP: Diagnose 2025 Underperformance

JP's GMS drop despite launch growth is the biggest red flag. Investigate: (1) seller category mix — are new JP DSR sellers in low-demand niches? (2) FBA adoption rate — JP heavily rewards FBA. (3) Is the 2024 spike explainable by a few outlier sellers?

3

AE: Double Down on Momentum

AE shows textbook ramp-up curve and is on track to surpass AU. Consider: increasing launch target, providing AE-specific selection guidance (climate/cultural fit), and establishing AE best-practice playbook from early successes.

4

AU: Steady Growth, Low Risk

AU grew +90% YoY to $1.08M with stable efficiency. Not a volume play (70 launches) but reliable returns. Maintain pace — no evidence of saturation yet given consistent month-over-month improvements.

5

Fix the Productivity Gap

Expansion's per-launch GMS ($21K) is 3.2x lower than NA ($67K). Priority actions: (1) improve first-sale-30d rate, (2) increase FBA/ads adoption guidance for Expansion sellers, (3) consider quality-gating launches that lack clear market-fit rather than pursuing volume alone.

Data Caveats

  • Expansion = EU + JP + AU + AE + SA (all non-NA marketplaces)
  • DSR channel only — excludes ESM and SSR launched sellers
  • GMS includes all active DSR sellers (all cohorts), not just current-year launches
  • JP 2024 spike may reflect a small number of high-performing sellers rather than broad-based growth
  • AE/SA effectively started in 2025; YoY% is meaningless — use absolute values
  • GMS in USD — JPY/EUR exchange rate fluctuations affect comparability across years
  • NA's dominance in GMS share partly reflects the US market's sheer size (not necessarily better execution)
